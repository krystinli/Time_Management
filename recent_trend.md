# Your August Check-In 🌻

![A cozy illustration of coding, focused work, and self-care in August](img/august_reflection.png)

*A little reflection using data through August 23.*

## The Big Picture

You did **lots of Development in August**—way more than in July. Your Development time grew from about 7.3 hours to 19.3 hours in a typical week. That is a 165% jump! You clearly spent this month learning, building, and investing in yourself. 🌱

Overall, you also logged more time. Once weekdays and weekends are compared fairly, your typical week went from **44.5 hours in July to 48.3 hours in August**, an 8.7% increase.

The biggest change was not simply “doing more,” though. You changed where your energy went:

- Work eased from 28.4 to 21.3 hours per typical week.
- Development jumped from 7.3 to 19.3 hours.
- Self-Care softened a little, from 8.7 to 7.7 hours.

So August felt much more like a **growth month** than a traditional work month.

## Weekdays and Weekends Felt Different

| Your average day | July | August |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Weekday | 6.89h | 7.17h |
| Weekend day | 5.00h | 6.25h |

Your weekdays stayed busy, but Development took a much bigger role. You averaged **2.77 hours of Development per weekday**, compared with just 0.94 hours in July.

Work moved the other way, from 5.41 to 4.27 hours per weekday. Weekday Self-Care also became very light at 0.13 hours per day, so most of your weekday energy went into Work and Development.

Your weekends became your **grow-and-reset space**:

- You averaged 2.75 hours of Development per weekend day.
- You averaged 3.50 hours of Self-Care per weekend day.
- You logged no Work hours on August weekends.

Almost all of your August Self-Care—28 of 30 hours—happened on weekends. You still made room for yourself, but you mostly saved it for Saturday and Sunday. 🫶

## A Gentle Thing to Notice

August started with a lot of energy, then gradually slowed down:

- Aug 3–9: 58 hours
- Aug 10–16: 48 hours
- Aug 17–23: 38 hours

That does not erase the strong month. August is still ahead of July overall. It just looks like you may have needed a breather after a very development-heavy start.

## Your August Story

You did more overall, learned a lot, and gave your weekends a clearer purpose. Development grew from a small slice of your time in July to almost the same size as Work in August.

The balance was not perfect: Work was below its usual target, and weekday Self-Care was easy to squeeze out. But the month still shows real progress. You made space to grow, protected your weekends from Work, and kept your total effort above your goal. 💗

## Small Nerdy Note

August is not finished yet, so comparing raw monthly totals would be unfair. The numbers above use a “typical week” made from five average weekdays and two average weekend days. That lets July and August be compared without the calendar getting in the way.

Your current goals add up to 36 hours in a typical week. August is running at 48.3 hours—about 12.3 hours above that combined goal.
